## Return Policy
- Gadgets and electronics: 14 days from the delivery date, item must be complete with all accessories.
- Grocery items: not returnable once delivered; contact support for damaged or missing items.
- Everything else: 30 days from delivery, unused and in original packaging.
- Opened items are accepted within the window; refunds are issued to the original payment method.
- Refunds appear 3-5 business days after approval.
- A prepaid return label is available for every eligible return.

## Shipping
- Standard: 3-5 business days, free on orders over $50.
- Expedited: 1-2 business days.
- Same-day: available in selected cities for eligible members.

## Warranty
Manufacturing defects are covered for the period stated on each product. Accidental damage and liquid
damage beyond the stated water-resistance rating are not covered.

## Loyalty Programme

### Tiers (lifetime points balance)
- Silver: 0 to 1,999 points
- Gold: 2,000 to 7,999 points
- Platinum: 8,000 points or more

### Earning points
- Standard items: 1 point per $1 paid
- Gadgets: 3 points per $1 paid
- Groceries: 4 points per $1 paid

### Redeeming points
- 50 points are worth $1.
- Points redeem in blocks of 250.
- Points can cover at most 40% of an order.
- The tier discount is applied after points are redeemed.

### Tier benefits
- Silver: standard shipping rates.
- Gold: free expedited shipping and an 8% member discount.
- Platinum: free same-day shipping (where available), a 12% member discount and priority support.

## Order Status Definitions
- PROCESSING: order confirmed and being prepared.
- SHIPPED: handed to the carrier.
- OUT FOR DELIVERY: with the local courier today.
- DELIVERED: delivery confirmed.
- CANCELLED: order cancelled before shipment.
